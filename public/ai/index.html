Why pilots stall here

African AI budgets are spent on experimentation, not production. The data is unlabelled, no one owns drift or evaluation, and compliance — the Data Protection Act 2019 and Kenya's National AI Strategy — is becoming a procurement gate rather than a courtesy.

Sources: PwC Africa AI study (1,217 executives, 2026) · Kenya National AI Strategy 2025–2030
